Privacy Policy — Salah Reminder

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Salah Reminder ("the app") is a prayer time and Qibla direction app. This page explains what information the app uses and how.

Location

The app asks for your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for where you are. This location is used only on your device to fetch prayer times from a prayer-times service and to calculate the Qibla bearing. It is not stored, logged, or shared with any third party by us.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app uses your device's notification system to alert you at prayer times. No notification data is sent to or stored on any server.

Third-party services

The app fetches prayer times from the AlAdhan API and, optionally, a location-naming service to show your city name. These requests include only your coordinates and are used solely to return prayer times or a place name; we do not control or store what these third parties log on their end.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers at this time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the app adds features (such as ads), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing for this app.
